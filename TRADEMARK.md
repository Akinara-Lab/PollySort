## What you may do without asking

- Use, modify, fork and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0, including commercially.
- Say truthfully that your project "uses PollySort", "is built on PollySort", or "is
  compatible with PollySort".
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and reviews.
- Keep the name in an unmodified redistribution (a package, a distro, a Docker image).

## What needs our written permission

- Naming a **modified** version "PollySort", or a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Using "PollySort" or "Akinara" in your product name, company name, or domain name.
- Using our logo or wordmark as your own branding.
- Anything that suggests Akinara endorses, sponsors or maintains your fork or service.

## If you fork

Forking is welcome — that is the point of Apache-2.0. Please rename it. A fork that keeps
the name confuses users about who supports it and who is responsible when something goes
wrong with their documents.

A good pattern: *"YourName, a fork of PollySort"*.
