# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What we expect

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience, background, identity or the
  quality of their first pull request.
- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, what would be better.
- Accept feedback the same way. Maintainers get things wrong too.
- Keep discussion in the open where possible, so others can learn from it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any space where someone is representing PollySort or Akinara.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Akinara-Lab/PollySort/security/advisories/new)
— it is the most private reporting channel GitHub offers and reaches only the maintainers.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will respond, and we will tell the reporter what
we did. Depending on severity, consequences range from a private word, to having a comment
removed, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

Maintainers who do not follow this document face the same consequences as anyone else.
